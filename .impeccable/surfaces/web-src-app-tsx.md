# Landing page

Scope: the public RENTrust landing page (`web/`). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: renters in Lagos and Abuja first; landlords and agents second. Job: understand in seconds what RENTrust is and how it protects them, then join the waitlist. Action: waitlist sign-up (email, role, city) in one form at the bottom of the page, composed as an email to the team until a form backend exists. Proof: the verification mechanism itself; no testimonials, counts or prices exist.

Content: short. Hero, the three checks, five steps from search to rent management, a brief landlord section, closing waitlist. The full workflow, fees table and data-protection list are cut.

## Direction contract

THESIS: RENTrust proves a rental the way a registered surveyor proves a plot, so the page is a survey plan. It refuses the category default of an apartment photo with a search bar over it.

OWN-WORLD: Cool drafting-film ground (#F1F5F3), navy technical-pen linework (#0E3B61), with green (#2A8651) reserved only for verified states. Beacons are small circles with IDs, edges carry bearings and distances, a framed title block, a north arrow, scale bars. Overpass in one family, with tabular figures for every number. Square sheet borders, hairline and 2px rules, no cards and no shadows.

STORY: Visitors learn that renters, landlords and each property are checked separately, see five steps from first search to rent day, so they understand RENTrust manages the whole rental, believe it because the mechanism is drawn, and join the waitlist.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full sheet framed by a drawing border. A large parcel boundary is centred on the sheet with beacons RT 01 to RT 06 and bearings on each edge. The headline, the slogan "From search to keys in one single trusted process." and the body are centred inside the plot, with a "Join the waitlist" button that scrolls to the single waitlist form at the bottom of the page. The north arrow and scale bar sit top-left.

FORM: Survey Plan, position 4 of the grounded list, seed key a8a5a834. Raises: one numeral form (nixie); each step shows what it unlocks next (algorave); leader lines to framed notes (anime); colour rationed by role (bauhaus); scale rises section by section (cityscape); "verified" only where all three checks overlap (silk). Signature interaction: on load the boundary traverses beacon to beacon, and each beacon turns green as it is checked, then the plot closes as verified.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
